HOTEL POLICIES

Check-in begins at 2:00 PM.

Check-out is at 12:00 PM.

Guests may request late checkout.

Late checkout is subject to availability and may attract an additional fee.

Smoking is prohibited inside guest rooms.

Lost property should be reported to the front desk.

Guests must present valid identification during check-in.

Visitors must be registered with the front desk.

The hotel does not disclose guest information to unauthorized individuals.

Refunds and billing disputes must be escalated to management.
